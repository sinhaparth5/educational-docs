## Theorems and proofs

`Theorem`, `Proof` and `Note` work in every post without an import. Math is typeset with KaTeX [@katex].

<Theorem title="Pythagoras">

For a right triangle with legs $a, b$ and hypotenuse $c$, $a^2 + b^2 = c^2$.

</Theorem>

<Proof>

Arrange four copies of the triangle inside a square of side $a + b$. The uncovered area is
$(a+b)^2 - 4 \cdot \tfrac{1}{2}ab = a^2 + b^2$, and it is also a square of side $c$.

</Proof>

<Theorem kind="Definition" title="Derivative">

$f'(x) = \lim_{h \to 0} \dfrac{f(x+h) - f(x)}{h}$, when the limit exists [@spivak2008, chap. 9].

</Theorem>
